# Compositing checklist

Print this or keep it open next to the two photos.

## Before you start

- [ ] **Same camera height.** Eye level with eye level. A selfie held above the head or a photo from below will not match an eye-level group photo, even with perfect light.
- [ ] **Same distance.** Group photos are usually taken from 2–4 m. A photo of the person taken by someone else from 2–3 m (or with the phone's 2× lens) matches far better than a close selfie, which makes faces rounder and noses larger.
- [ ] **Same framing or more.** Head-to-toe group photo → you need legs and feet (by hand) or an AI tool that can draw them. Waist-up group photo → head and shoulders is enough.
- [ ] **Light from the same side.** Compare the bright side of the faces.
- [ ] **Same light quality.** Crisp midday shadows vs soft shade or overcast.
- [ ] **Similar color temperature.** Warm sunset, cool shade, yellow indoor bulbs. Skin tones show a mismatch first.
- [ ] **Sharp face.** No sunglasses, no beauty filter, not in profile unless the group is.

## Placement

- [ ] At the end of a row, half behind someone's shoulder, or in a natural gap.
- [ ] Eyes on the same horizontal line as other people standing on the same ground (see [horizon and scale](docs/02-horizon-and-scale.md)).
- [ ] A little lower and a little smaller if they stand further back.
- [ ] Some overlap with a neighbour. A person alone in empty space reads as pasted in.

## Finishing

- [ ] Contact shadow under the feet, in the same direction as the others' shadows.
- [ ] Soft shadow on the neighbour if they are close.
- [ ] Grain/noise matched (zoom to 100%).
- [ ] Edge check around the hair at 200%.
- [ ] Everyone else's face unchanged.

More context and a comparison of methods: [How to add a person to a photo](https://addpersontophoto.org/how-to-add-a-person-to-a-photo).
