# 5. Doing it by hand: layer-by-layer

The steps are the same in Photoshop, the free GIMP or the free browser editor Photopea.

1. Open the group photo, then the photo of the person in a second tab.
2. Select the person with a subject or object selection tool; refine the edges around the hair.
3. Copy, paste into the group photo as a new layer.
4. Scale so their eyes sit on the horizon line and their feet on the ground where they stand ([horizon and scale](02-horizon-and-scale.md)).
5. Add a layer mask and tuck them partly behind someone's shoulder.
6. Match brightness and contrast with Levels or Curves; match color with Color Balance, comparing skin tones side by side.
7. On a new layer under the person, paint the ground and contact shadow with a soft black brush at low opacity, in the same direction as the others' shadows.
8. If they are close to a neighbour, paint a faint shadow on the neighbour.
9. Add noise/grain to the person's layer until it matches at 100% zoom.
10. Check the hair edge at 200%, then flatten a copy and view the whole photo small (as a thumbnail): mismatches in light direction show up best at small size.

## Time and cost

| Method | Typical time | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| By hand in GIMP or Photopea | 30 minutes to a few hours | Free |
| Photoshop Generative Fill | 30 minutes to a few hours | Adobe subscription |
| Hiring a retoucher | 1–3 days | Per photo, set by the seller |
| AI tool built for this ([addpersontophoto.org](https://addpersontophoto.org)) | About a minute | First photo free with an account, then from $4.99 for 5 photos |

Whatever the method, the checks in [CHECKLIST.md](../CHECKLIST.md) decide how real the result looks.
